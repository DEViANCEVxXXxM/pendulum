About this model
Two point masses on massless rigid rods in a vertical plane under uniform gravity. Numerical integration uses fixed 1/240-second RK4 steps. The comparison starts with Δθ₁ only; it demonstrates sensitive dependence on initial conditions, not quantum uncertainty.
With damping set to zero, total energy should remain approximately constant up to numerical error. With damping, energy decreases physically, so the readout shows total energy change, not solely solver drift. The phase portrait plots θ₁ against angular velocity ω₁, with wrapped angles at ±180°.
